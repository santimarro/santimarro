### About Me

AI Lead at Santex, where I run the AI team driving the company's shift from software services to AI consulting. PhD in Artificial Intelligence focused on Natural Language Processing. I design and ship agentic systems, internal AI platforms, and LLM-powered developer tooling — with a strong bias toward AI that delivers measurable business outcomes, not demos.

- AI Lead Specialist @ **Santex**
- PhD in AI · Natural Language Processing
- Based in Córdoba, Argentina

### What I Work On

- **Agentic AI platforms** — internal platform that standardizes AI use cases across the company: skill catalog, RAG pipelines, role-based access.
- **LLM-powered code analytics** — evaluating pull requests at scale with LLMs to surface impact, clarity, and actionable feedback for dev teams.
- **AI-assisted SDLC** — Claude Code adoption at company scale: skills, MCP integrations, multi-agent workflows, compliance gates for AI tooling.
- **Applied NLP** — semantic search and matching, document understanding, text-to-SQL over data lakes.
